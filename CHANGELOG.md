# Changelog

## Unreleased

Initial playable Pose Party prototype, currently versioned as `0.1.0` in `package.json`.

- A 60-second webcam challenge with one second of valid matching per pose.
- 20 randomized upper-body poses, including six head-tilt combinations.
- Standing-position guidance and brief tracking-loss recovery without scoring unseen movement.
- English and Chinese UI, full-screen mode, and game sound effects.
- Three credited instrumental recordings and support for selecting local audio files.
- Worker-based pose inference, model warm-up, and stale-result protection.
- Local performance reports with exportable timing and tracking summaries.
- GitHub Pages deployment workflow with project-path support.

A public release and deployment have not yet been recorded here.
